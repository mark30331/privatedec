# NIW Recommendation Letter Template

Type: Manager / Senior Engineer / Industry Recommender  
Use case: recommender can speak to execution, engineering quality, security awareness, and practical impact.

Important: this is a drafting template only. The recommender should personalize it substantially.

To Whom It May Concern:

I am pleased to support Mr. Mark Kportufe and his National Interest Waiver petition. I served as [manager / senior engineer / technical lead] at [company], where I worked with Mr. Kportufe during his time as a software engineer. In that capacity, I had the opportunity to observe his technical abilities, execution discipline, and problem-solving skills in a real engineering environment.

Mr. Kportufe is a highly capable software engineer with strong backend, cloud, and systems skills. During his work at [company], he contributed to backend services, deployment automation, and data-processing workflows using technologies such as Python, Flask, Docker, AWS, DynamoDB, and CI/CD tooling. He demonstrated the ability to design and implement practical solutions that improved reliability, reduced manual effort, and supported production-oriented use cases. In my experience, he combines technical depth with a strong sense of ownership and system thinking.

Based on what I know of his current work, I believe Mr. Kportufe is applying those same strengths to an endeavor of clear public importance. He is developing `Privatedec`, a privacy-preserving platform that helps organizations prepare sensitive structured datasets for lower-risk research, analytics, and AI model development. This kind of work is increasingly important in the United States, especially in sectors such as healthcare, where sensitive data is valuable for innovation but difficult to use safely.

What makes his effort persuasive to me is that it addresses the real operational side of privacy-preserving data use. In practice, responsible data use is not solved by a single algorithm. It requires secure handling, structured workflows, controlled access, retention rules, reproducible outputs, and technical implementation that users can actually adopt. Mr. Kportufe's engineering background makes him well suited to build these kinds of systems in a practical and scalable way.

I also believe he is well positioned to advance this endeavor because he has already demonstrated the ability to build working systems, improve delivery processes, and think carefully about reliability and security. In my experience, those qualities are essential for work that aims to support sensitive data use in real organizations. His prior engineering and testing background gives him a sound foundation for creating trustworthy software infrastructure.

In my opinion, the United States would benefit from allowing Mr. Kportufe to continue this endeavor without restricting it to a single job offer or employer. The benefit of privacy-preserving data infrastructure is broader than one company or role. Systems in this area can support researchers, institutions, and organizations across the country, particularly as data-driven healthcare and responsible AI continue to grow in importance.

For these reasons, I strongly support Mr. Kportufe's NIW petition. I believe his endeavor is nationally important, that he is well positioned to advance it, and that his continued work in this area would provide meaningful benefit to the United States.

Sincerely,

[Name]  
[Title]  
[Company]

## Recommender Notes

Strong manager letters often include:

- a concrete example of technical work Mark performed well
- evidence of reliability, initiative, or ownership
- comments on security awareness or system quality
- explanation of why his endeavor has value beyond one company
