# NIW Recommendation Letter Template

Type: Independent Expert  
Use case: recommender is not your direct supervisor and can speak credibly about the national importance of your endeavor.

Important: this is a drafting template only. The recommender should revise it heavily and write in their own voice.

To Whom It May Concern:

I am pleased to write this letter in support of Mr. Mark Kportufe and his National Interest Waiver petition. I am [title / background / credentials], and my work focuses on [healthcare research / privacy / AI / cybersecurity / data governance]. Based on my experience in this field, I am well qualified to evaluate the significance of Mr. Kportufe's proposed endeavor and his ability to advance it.

I have reviewed Mr. Kportufe's background and his work developing `Privatedec`, a privacy-preserving software platform designed to help institutions prepare sensitive structured datasets for lower-risk sharing, analysis, and AI model development. In my view, this work addresses a problem of substantial merit and national importance. Healthcare organizations, researchers, and data-driven institutions in the United States routinely face major barriers when trying to use sensitive data responsibly. These barriers include privacy risk, regulatory and governance burdens, difficulty controlling access, and concern about re-identification. The result is often slower research, reduced collaboration, and underuse of valuable data resources.

Mr. Kportufe's work directly addresses this challenge. `Privatedec` is not merely a theoretical proposal. It is a practical technical system that combines secure dataset upload, anonymization workflows, configurable privacy controls, signed downloads, retention limits, and reproducible reporting. This combination is important because privacy-preserving work in healthcare is not only about algorithms; it is also about operational controls, repeatable governance, and implementation in real systems. In that respect, Mr. Kportufe's endeavor has real public value for healthcare research, clinical analytics, and responsible AI training.

I also believe Mr. Kportufe is well positioned to advance this endeavor. His academic and technical record is unusually strong. He has a Bachelor of Science in Software Engineering and is completing a Master of Science in Information Systems at the University of Utah with excellent academic performance. He also has direct engineering experience building backend systems, data-processing workflows, and cloud-based services using technologies such as Python, Django, Flask, Docker, AWS, PostgreSQL, and related tools. This combination of formal training and hands-on execution is highly relevant to the development of privacy-preserving healthcare data infrastructure.

In addition, Mr. Kportufe's background reflects more than narrow coding ability. His experience includes security-oriented work, including identifying a critical vulnerability in a prior role, as well as teaching and mentoring in database and programming contexts. These experiences strengthen my view that he can not only build technically sound systems, but also contribute to broader capacity-building and responsible use of data systems in the United States.

In my opinion, it would benefit the United States to allow Mr. Kportufe to continue advancing this endeavor without limiting it to a single job offer or employer-specific position. The importance of privacy-preserving healthcare data infrastructure extends far beyond one company. Tools and systems in this area can support hospitals, research centers, universities, public-interest projects, and AI development efforts across the country. Because of the broad social and technical value of this work, I believe Mr. Kportufe's continued efforts will provide meaningful benefits to the United States.

For these reasons, I strongly support Mr. Kportufe's NIW petition. I believe his endeavor has substantial merit and national importance, that he is well positioned to advance it, and that the United States would benefit from allowing him to continue this work.

Sincerely,

[Recommender Name]  
[Title]  
[Institution / Organization]

## Recommender Notes

The recommender should try to add:

- 1-2 sentences explaining why privacy-preserving healthcare data use matters in their own field
- 1 paragraph explaining why Mark's work is technically credible
- 1 paragraph explaining why the endeavor benefits the U.S. beyond a single employer
- concrete facts about the recommender's own expertise so USCIS sees why the opinion matters
