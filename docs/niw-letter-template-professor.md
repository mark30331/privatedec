# NIW Recommendation Letter Template

Type: Professor / Academic Recommender  
Use case: recommender can speak to your academic strength, technical depth, research promise, and national value.

Important: this is a drafting template only. The recommender should adapt it in their own words.

To Whom It May Concern:

I am writing in strong support of Mr. Mark Kportufe and his National Interest Waiver petition. I am [title] in [department / field] at [institution], and my work includes [databases / information systems / AI / cybersecurity / healthcare informatics]. I am familiar with Mr. Kportufe through [teaching / advising / academic supervision / review of his work], and I have had the opportunity to assess both his technical ability and the significance of his proposed endeavor.

Mr. Kportufe stands out as an exceptionally strong technical candidate. He earned a Bachelor of Science in Software Engineering with high academic distinction and is completing a Master of Science in Information Systems at the University of Utah with outstanding academic performance. In my experience, students who combine this level of academic rigor with real implementation ability are relatively uncommon. Mr. Kportufe has shown that he can move from abstract technical concepts to functioning systems with practical value.

His proposed endeavor centers on the development of `Privatedec`, a privacy-preserving platform for preparing sensitive datasets for research, analytics, and AI-related use. This work has substantial merit and national importance. Healthcare institutions and researchers increasingly depend on data for clinical research, operational analytics, and model development, yet privacy concerns and governance burdens often limit the safe use of such data. A system that supports structured anonymization, controlled access, retention limits, and reproducible reporting can play an important role in enabling safer, more responsible use of sensitive datasets in the United States.

What makes Mr. Kportufe's work particularly compelling is that it is both technically grounded and operationally useful. His system incorporates real software engineering components rather than abstract academic claims alone. It reflects backend engineering, cloud deployment, data processing, and governance design. In my opinion, this is exactly the sort of practical innovation that can help bridge the gap between technical research concepts and actual use in institutional settings.

I also believe Mr. Kportufe is well positioned to advance this endeavor because of his broader record. He has practical industry experience in software engineering, cloud-based services, and automation. He also has teaching experience in databases and programming, which demonstrates both command of the subject matter and the ability to communicate technical ideas clearly. These qualities are important for an applicant whose work has the potential to influence not only software systems, but also how others adopt and use those systems responsibly.

In my opinion, the United States would benefit from allowing Mr. Kportufe to continue this work without requiring that it be tied to a single employer through the labor certification process. The value of privacy-preserving data infrastructure extends across sectors, institutions, and research environments. His endeavor is broader than one position and has the potential to support data-intensive work of public importance, especially in healthcare research and responsible AI development.

I therefore strongly support Mr. Kportufe's NIW petition. I believe his proposed endeavor is nationally important, that he has the technical and academic background to advance it, and that his continued work in this area would benefit the United States.

Sincerely,

[Professor Name]  
[Title / Department]  
[University]

## Recommender Notes

Helpful additions:

- mention any class, project, or TA work you supervised
- mention his grades, rigor, or problem-solving ability if you know them
- explain why privacy-preserving healthcare data systems matter academically or socially
- connect his work to research enablement, responsible AI, or data governance
