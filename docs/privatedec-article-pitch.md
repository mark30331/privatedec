## Short Abstract

Healthcare organizations increasingly depend on structured data for research, analytics, and AI, yet many teams still rely on informal or poorly documented preparation workflows. This article introduces `Privatedec`, a Django-based platform for privacy-preserving preparation of tabular datasets through configurable anonymization, secure result delivery, retention-aware handling, and reproducible reporting. The piece argues that privacy-preserving data use should be treated as infrastructure rather than a one-time preprocessing step.
