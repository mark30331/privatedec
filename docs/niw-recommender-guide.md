# NIW Recommender Guide

Purpose: help recommenders write strong, specific NIW letters for Mark Kportufe.

Important: recommenders should write in their own words. This guide is only to make their letters more concrete and useful.

## What The Recommender Should Emphasize

The best letters do not simply say Mark is intelligent or hardworking. They explain:

1. who the recommender is and why their opinion matters
2. how they know Mark or how they evaluated his work
3. what Mark is building
4. why that work matters to the United States
5. why Mark is particularly well positioned to advance it
6. why his work has value beyond one employer

## Suggested Recommender Types

- healthcare researcher working with sensitive datasets
- faculty member in information systems, databases, or data science
- privacy, cybersecurity, or compliance professional
- engineering manager or senior software architect
- AI / ML practitioner who understands data governance risks

## What Makes A Letter Strong

- specific examples instead of generic praise
- clear statement of why privacy-preserving healthcare data infrastructure matters
- explanation that Mark's work supports research and responsible AI
- clear view that his endeavor is broader than one employer
- language about national importance, not just personal success

## Sample Topics A Recommender Could Address

- healthcare research often stalls because sensitive data is difficult to share safely
- privacy-preserving tools can reduce barriers to collaboration
- AI systems are only as responsible as the data governance surrounding training data
- Mark has the technical mix needed to build practical, production-oriented privacy tooling
- Mark's background in cloud systems, backend engineering, and data systems is directly relevant

## Suggested Letter Structure

1. Introduction
   - name, title, organization, expertise
   - why the recommender is qualified to assess Mark

2. Relationship or basis of evaluation
   - how they know Mark, or how they reviewed his work

3. Description of Mark's endeavor
   - describe Privatedec and the broader privacy-preserving healthcare research mission

4. National importance
   - explain why this problem matters in the U.S.

5. Why Mark is well positioned
   - discuss his education, engineering record, projects, security awareness, and execution ability

6. Conclusion
   - recommend favorable consideration

## Sample Language Snippets

These are not for copy-paste use as-is. They are prompts the recommender can adapt.

- "In my opinion, Mr. Kportufe's work addresses a nationally important problem: enabling safer, more practical use of sensitive healthcare data for research and advanced analytics."
- "What distinguishes his work is that he is not merely proposing a theoretical privacy method; he is building an operational platform that combines technical privacy controls with governance mechanisms such as retention limits, controlled access, and reproducible reporting."
- "Given his academic excellence, practical engineering background, and demonstrated ability to build cloud-based data systems, I believe he is well positioned to advance this endeavor."
- "The benefit of this work extends beyond any one employer because privacy-preserving data infrastructure can support researchers, institutions, and innovation ecosystems across the United States."

## What Recommenders Should Avoid

- vague praise with no examples
- statements that sound like immigration legal conclusions
- overstating compliance claims
- saying the tool guarantees legal de-identification in every context
