# NIW Endeavor Statement

Author: Mark Kportufe  
Project: Privatedec  
Focus Area: Privacy-preserving healthcare research infrastructure and safer dataset preparation for AI and analytics

## Working Positioning Statement

I am developing `Privatedec`, a privacy-preserving software platform that helps healthcare researchers, data teams, and institutions prepare sensitive datasets for lower-risk sharing, analysis, and AI training. The platform is designed to reduce re-identification risk through structured anonymization workflows, secure storage, signed delivery, retention limits, and reproducible reporting.

My proposed endeavor is to advance technical infrastructure that enables safer use of healthcare and research data while supporting scientific collaboration, operational efficiency, and responsible AI development in the United States.

## Why This Endeavor Has Substantial Merit and National Importance

Healthcare institutions and researchers face a persistent challenge: valuable clinical and operational datasets are often difficult to share or reuse because of privacy risk, governance burdens, and fear of unauthorized disclosure. This challenge slows research, weakens collaboration, and complicates the use of data for public-interest goals such as clinical analytics, biomedical research, and AI model development.

`Privatedec` addresses this problem by giving teams a practical workflow for:

- uploading structured datasets securely
- identifying quasi-identifiers and sensitive attributes
- applying privacy controls such as k-anonymity, l-diversity, and t-closeness
- generating reproducible result reports
- enforcing data minimization through retention and deletion policies
- controlling result access through signed downloads

This work has substantial merit because it supports safer data use in healthcare research and responsible AI development. It has national importance because privacy-preserving data infrastructure can improve the ability of institutions across the United States to collaborate, analyze sensitive information, reduce breach exposure, and unlock socially valuable research without relying on unsafe ad hoc processes.
